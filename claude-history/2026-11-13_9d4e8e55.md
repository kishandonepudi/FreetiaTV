# M3U IPTV Player ("TuneTV")

Build a free, ad-free IPTV player for Android phones/tablets, Android TV, and Fire TV. It plays channels from user-supplied M3U/M3U8 playlist URLs. The app bundles no channels, links to no providers, and collects no personal data. Design it like a familiar TV: one remote, big text, predictable buttons, and no setup beyond pasting a URL. It must work for children, adults, and seniors.

## 1. Platforms and Tech

- **Targets:** One codebase with runtime-detected TV and mobile layouts. Support Android 8.0 (API 26) and later, Android TV, and Fire TV (Fire OS).future maintainable with simple and open source easy to maintain for future release and security not vulnerable also considering all guidelines for playstore and firetv deployments
- **UI:** Compose for TV (`androidx.tv`) on TV, Compose Material 3 on mobile. Shared business logic.
- **Playback:** Media3/ExoPlayer for HLS, DASH, and MPEG-TS, with adaptive track selection and hardware decoding.
- **Storage:** Room for playlists, channels, EPG, favourites, groups, locks, and reminders. DataStore for settings. Encrypted storage for the PIN hash, recovery code, and playlist URLs (URLs can contain tokens).
- **Background work:** WorkManager for playlist and EPG refresh. AlarmManager for reminders and the sleep timer. A foreground service with a media session for background play.

## 2. Navigation and Remote UX (Core Requirement)

- Full operation with D-pad (Up, Down, Left, Right, OK/Enter, Back). No touch required on TV.
- **Player:** Up/Down changes channel. OK opens the channel list. Number keys jump to a channel by number, with a short entry timeout.
- **Color buttons** (red, green, yellow, blue) map to Favourites, Search, EPG, and Settings. Make them customisable.
- **Back in the player:** one Back returns to the previous channel only if that setting is on (off by default; when on, use a long-press Back). Two Backs within 2 seconds fully exit the app. After the first Back, show a toast: "Press Back again to exit."
- Large focus outlines, body text at least 24sp, touch and focus targets at least 48dp, and a high-contrast mode for seniors.
- No screen is more than two levels deep from Home.
- Every screen shows a hint bar, for example "OK: Watch  ↑↓: Browse  Back: Return."
- Clean, modern, interactive visuals: focus animations, channel logos, and a non-intrusive on-screen info banner.

## 3. Features

### 3.1 Playlists (save N playlists with priority)
- Save any number of playlists. No fixed cap. Lists are virtualised so performance depends on channel count, not playlist count. Test with 50 playlists.
- Each playlist stores: name, source URL, region, priority number, default flag, "Update when app starts" (on/off), background-refresh interval, "Allow background play" (on/off), and "Resume last channel" (on/off).
- Priority is a numbered sequence (1 = highest). Users reorder with D-pad (Move Up/Move Down on the item) or drag on mobile.
- **Startup rules:**
  - One playlist: open it directly.
  - Several: open the default playlist if one is set, otherwise priority 1.
  - If "Resume last channel" is on, tune to the last channel in that playlist. Otherwise, tune to the first channel.
- **Switching:** A dedicated "Playlists" key or menu item switches sources without leaving the player.
- **Failure:** If the chosen playlist fails, fall back to the next priority, show a message naming the failed playlist, and never block the UI.
- **Views:** Show separate lists per playlist, plus a merged "All" view in priority order.

### 3.2 Auto-update on app start (per playlist)
- Each playlist has an "Update when app starts" toggle, set during configuration. Default on for the first playlist, off for the rest.
- Stale-while-revalidate: show cached channels immediately, then update in the background. Never make the user wait for a download.
- If changes are found, show a non-blocking toast with the count of added and removed channels.
- If the update fails, keep the cached copy and show a small warning icon on that playlist.
- Skip the update if the last successful update was less than 10 minutes ago.
- A playlist with auto-update off makes no network request on startup.

### 3.3 Regions and favourites
- Parse `tvg-country`, `tvg-language`, and `group-title` to build region and category filters.
- Region picker on first run, changeable later.
- Global favourites plus user-created groups (for example "Kids", "News", "Family").
- Recently Watched list (last 20 channels).

### 3.4 Audio and captions
- Detect multiple audio tracks and show a language list in the player menu. Remember the choice per channel. Fall back to the default track if the preferred one is missing.
- CC on/off per channel, remembered. Support embedded CEA-608/708 and sidecar subtitles.
- Caption size, colour, and background in Settings.

### 3.5 Resolution and auto-quality
- Resolution menu: Auto, then the variants the stream offers (1080p, 720p, 480p, and so on).
- In Auto, ExoPlayer adapts bitrate. On a drop due to slow network, show a non-blocking toast: "Network slow. Switched to 480p." Restore quality automatically when bandwidth recovers, with a matching toast.
- Options: turn quality-change notifications off, or "Lock quality."

### 3.6 Buffer
- Presets: Low Latency (about 2 s), Balanced (about 10 s), Stable (about 30 s), and Custom (min/max seconds).
- Changes apply on the next tune-in. The info banner shows buffer health.

### 3.7 Child Safe Mode and PIN
- PIN set on first enablement. Stored as a salted hash. Required to turn off Child Safe Mode or change safety settings.
- Recovery: a recovery code shown once at PIN setup, plus a security question as an alternative.
- Adult channels are detected from group titles and keywords ("adult", "18+"), plus a manual "Mark as adult" option.
- With Child Safe Mode on, adult channels are hidden from lists, search, and channel up/down.

### 3.8 Channel lock
- Lock individual channels or bulk-lock by group or region. Separate from Child Safe Mode.
- Locked channels show a padlock and require the PIN to tune in. They are skipped by channel up/down unless unlocked.
- Session unlock: one PIN entry unlocks for a configurable period (default 30 minutes, or until exit). Use a TV-sized number pad.

### 3.9 Full EPG
- XMLTV, from the playlist's `x-tvg-url` header or a user-supplied URL.
- Full-screen grid with D-pad time and channel scrolling.
- Search by programme title, channel, and genre/category. Region filter.
- Now/next in the info banner.
- **Reminders:** set on a future programme. At start time, show a notification. If enabled, wake the screen and tune to the channel. Use full-screen intents and explain the permissions the user must grant. Reminders list in Settings for review and cancel.
- Catch-up/timeshift where the playlist provides catch-up tags (can be v2).

### 3.10 Sleep timer
- Toggle on/off. Default 60 minutes when on. Configurable from 10 to 240 minutes in steps.
- At 1 minute remaining, show an overlay: "Sleeping in 60 seconds. Press OK to continue."
- On expiry: pause, dim, and perform a full exit (including cache clear). Put the device to sleep if the platform allows.
- Quick menu to add 15 or 30 minutes. Keeps working in the background.

### 3.11 Background play and auto-start on TV
- **Background play** (per playlist): when Home is pressed and the option is on, audio continues with a notification showing play, pause, and stop. On TV, video stops unless picture-in-picture is supported. Give a one-action return path from the notification.
- Home without Background Play stops playback. Home never clears the cache.
- **Auto-start on TV:** "Open app when TV turns on." Off by default. Uses a `BOOT_COMPLETED` receiver and a foreground service, opening the default playlist and its last channel.
- Android 10+ restricts background activity launches. On some Fire TV and Android TV builds, auto-start works only if the user enables it in system settings. Detect this and show a one-time setup screen with steps. Test on each target device before promising the feature. Force-stopped apps cannot auto-start; this is an OS rule.

### 3.12 Exit
- Double-Back (within 2 seconds), or "Exit app" in Settings: stop playback, close the media session and service, run the exit cache clear, and close the process.
- Sleep timer end: same as full exit.

### 3.13 Cache policy
- **Cleared on full exit and on cold start** (each is a separate toggle, both on by default, with a "Clear now" button in Settings):
  - Logo and thumbnail cache.
  - EPG download and parse buffers (parsed EPG stays in the database).
  - Stream probe and health-check results.
  - Temporary files, player segment cache, and temp crash logs.
- **Never cleared:** Settings, PIN hash, recovery code, saved playlists and their order and options, channels, favourites, groups, locks, and reminders.
- **Logo cache cap:** 50 MB, with LRU eviction. Startup clear removes logo files older than the cap policy allows. This keeps the first screen fast on slow networks while meeting the cache-clear requirement.

### 3.14 Settings (everything configurable)
- **Playback:** buffer, default quality, auto-quality, aspect ratio, subtitle style, default audio language.
- **Startup:** playlist to open, resume last channel, Home or Player on launch, auto-start on TV.
- **Navigation:** Back behaviour (default off), channel-change method (instant or OSD delay), number-entry timeout, color-button mapping.
- **Display:** theme, text size, high contrast, OSD timeout (default 5 s).
- **Safety:** Child Safe Mode, PIN, recovery, default lock behaviour, session-unlock duration.
- **Sleep and reminders:** sleep default, reminder lead time, reminders list.
- **Playlists and EPG:** manage playlists and priority, refresh interval, EPG source, clear cache.
- **Cache:** clear on exit toggle, clear on startup toggle, logo cache size, Clear now.
- **Advanced:** export/import settings and playlist URLs (file or QR), diagnostics (codec, bitrate, dropped frames, "Send report"), reset, Exit app.

### 3.15 Other
- Number-key channel entry and last-channel toggle.
- Info banner (channel name, number, logo, now/next, resolution, buffer state). Appears on change and on OK.
- Playlist health check: flag dead streams, with an option to hide them.
- Auto-retry on stream failure (a few tries, then the next variant), with a clear message instead of a blank screen.
- Voice search on Fire TV and Android TV where supported.
- Phone-only: picture-in-picture and background audio with lock-screen controls.

- Privacy: no account, no analytics unless opted in, no ads.
- Profiles (v1.1): per-person favourites, recents, and safety level. Child profiles always have Child Safe Mode on.

# M3U IPTV Player ("TuneTV")

Build a free, ad-free IPTV player for Android phones/tablets, Android TV, and Fire TV. It plays channels from user-supplied M3U/M3U8 playlist URLs. The app bundles no channels, links to no providers, and collects no personal data. Design it like a familiar TV: one remote, big text, predictable buttons, and no setup beyond pasting a URL. It must work for children, adults, and seniors.

## 1. Platforms and Tech

- **Targets:** One codebase with runtime-detected TV and mobile layouts. Support Android 8.0 (API 26) and later, Android TV, and Fire TV (Fire OS).future maintainable with simple and open source easy to maintain for future release and security not vulnerable also considering all guidelines for playstore and firetv deployments
- **UI:** Compose for TV (`androidx.tv`) on TV, Compose Material 3 on mobile. Shared business logic.
- **Playback:** Media3/ExoPlayer for HLS, DASH, and MPEG-TS, with adaptive track selection and hardware decoding.
- **Storage:** Room for playlists, channels, EPG, favourites, groups, locks, and reminders. DataStore for settings. Encrypted storage for the PIN hash, recovery code, and playlist URLs (URLs can contain tokens).
- **Background work:** WorkManager for playlist and EPG refresh. AlarmManager for reminders and the sleep timer. A foreground service with a media session for background play.

## 2. Navigation and Remote UX (Core Requirement)

- Full operation with D-pad (Up, Down, Left, Right, OK/Enter, Back). No touch required on TV.
- **Player:** Up/Down changes channel. OK opens the channel list. Number keys jump to a channel by number, with a short entry timeout.
- **Color buttons** (red, green, yellow, blue) map to Favourites, Search, EPG, and Settings. Make them customisable.
- **Back in the player:** one Back returns to the previous channel only if that setting is on (off by default; when on, use a long-press Back). Two Backs within 2 seconds fully exit the app. After the first Back, show a toast: "Press Back again to exit."
- Large focus outlines, body text at least 24sp, touch and focus targets at least 48dp, and a high-contrast mode for seniors.
- No screen is more than two levels deep from Home.
- Every screen shows a hint bar, for example "OK: Watch  ↑↓: Browse  Back: Return."
- Clean, modern, interactive visuals: focus animations, channel logos, and a non-intrusive on-screen info banner.

## 3. Features

### 3.1 Playlists (save N playlists with priority)
- Save any number of playlists. No fixed cap. Lists are virtualised so performance depends on channel count, not playlist count. Test with 50 playlists.
- Each playlist stores: name, source URL, region, priority number, default flag, "Update when app starts" (on/off), background-refresh interval, "Allow background play" (on/off), and "Resume last channel" (on/off).
- Priority is a numbered sequence (1 = highest). Users reorder with D-pad (Move Up/Move Down on the item) or drag on mobile.
- **Startup rules:**
  - One playlist: open it directly.
  - Several: open the default playlist if one is set, otherwise priority 1.
  - If "Resume last channel" is on, tune to the last channel in that playlist. Otherwise, tune to the first channel.
- **Switching:** A dedicated "Playlists" key or menu item switches sources without leaving the player.
- **Failure:** If the chosen playlist fails, fall back to the next priority, show a message naming the failed playlist, and never block the UI.
- **Views:** Show separate lists per playlist, plus a merged "All" view in priority order.

### 3.2 Auto-update on app start (per playlist)
- Each playlist has an "Update when app starts" toggle, set during configuration. Default on for the first playlist, off for the rest.
- Stale-while-revalidate: show cached channels immediately, then update in the background. Never make the user wait for a download.
- If changes are found, show a non-blocking toast with the count of added and removed channels.
- If the update fails, keep the cached copy and show a small warning icon on that playlist.
- Skip the update if the last successful update was less than 10 minutes ago.
- A playlist with auto-update off makes no network request on startup.

### 3.3 Regions and favourites
- Parse `tvg-country`, `tvg-language`, and `group-title` to build region and category filters.
- Region picker on first run, changeable later.
- Global favourites plus user-created groups (for example "Kids", "News", "Family").
- Recently Watched list (last 20 channels).

### 3.4 Audio and captions
- Detect multiple audio tracks and show a language list in the player menu. Remember the choice per channel. Fall back to the default track if the preferred one is missing.
- CC on/off per channel, remembered. Support embedded CEA-608/708 and sidecar subtitles.
- Caption size, colour, and background in Settings.

### 3.5 Resolution and auto-quality
- Resolution menu: Auto, then the variants the stream offers (1080p, 720p, 480p, and so on).
- In Auto, ExoPlayer adapts bitrate. On a drop due to slow network, show a non-blocking toast: "Network slow. Switched to 480p." Restore quality automatically when bandwidth recovers, with a matching toast.
- Options: turn quality-change notifications off, or "Lock quality."

### 3.6 Buffer
- Presets: Low Latency (about 2 s), Balanced (about 10 s), Stable (about 30 s), and Custom (min/max seconds).
- Changes apply on the next tune-in. The info banner shows buffer health.

### 3.7 Child Safe Mode and PIN
- PIN set on first enablement. Stored as a salted hash. Required to turn off Child Safe Mode or change safety settings.
- Recovery: a recovery code shown once at PIN setup, plus a security question as an alternative.
- Adult channels are detected from group titles and keywords ("adult", "18+"), plus a manual "Mark as adult" option.
- With Child Safe Mode on, adult channels are hidden from lists, search, and channel up/down.

### 3.8 Channel lock
- Lock individual channels or bulk-lock by group or region. Separate from Child Safe Mode.
- Locked channels show a padlock and require the PIN to tune in. They are skipped by channel up/down unless unlocked.
- Session unlock: one PIN entry unlocks for a configurable period (default 30 minutes, or until exit). Use a TV-sized number pad.

### 3.9 Full EPG
- XMLTV, from the playlist's `x-tvg-url` header or a user-supplied URL.
- Full-screen grid with D-pad time and channel scrolling.
- Search by programme title, channel, and genre/category. Region filter.
- Now/next in the info banner.
- **Reminders:** set on a future programme. At start time, show a notification. If enabled, wake the screen and tune to the channel. Use full-screen intents and explain the permissions the user must grant. Reminders list in Settings for review and cancel.
- Catch-up/timeshift where the playlist provides catch-up tags (can be v2).

### 3.10 Sleep timer
- Toggle on/off. Default 60 minutes when on. Configurable from 10 to 240 minutes in steps.
- At 1 minute remaining, show an overlay: "Sleeping in 60 seconds. Press OK to continue."
- On expiry: pause, dim, and perform a full exit (including cache clear). Put the device to sleep if the platform allows.
- Quick menu to add 15 or 30 minutes. Keeps working in the background.

### 3.11 Background play and auto-start on TV
- **Background play** (per playlist): when Home is pressed and the option is on, audio continues with a notification showing play, pause, and stop. On TV, video stops unless picture-in-picture is supported. Give a one-action return path from the notification.
- Home without Background Play stops playback. Home never clears the cache.
- **Auto-start on TV:** "Open app when TV turns on." Off by default. Uses a `BOOT_COMPLETED` receiver and a foreground service, opening the default playlist and its last channel.
- Android 10+ restricts background activity launches. On some Fire TV and Android TV builds, auto-start works only if the user enables it in system settings. Detect this and show a one-time setup screen with steps. Test on each target device before promising the feature. Force-stopped apps cannot auto-start; this is an OS rule.

### 3.12 Exit
- Double-Back (within 2 seconds), or "Exit app" in Settings: stop playback, close the media session and service, run the exit cache clear, and close the process.
- Sleep timer end: same as full exit.

### 3.13 Cache policy
- **Cleared on full exit and on cold start** (each is a separate toggle, both on by default, with a "Clear now" button in Settings):
  - Logo and thumbnail cache.
  - EPG download and parse buffers (parsed EPG stays in the database).
  - Stream probe and health-check results.
  - Temporary files, player segment cache, and temp crash logs.
- **Never cleared:** Settings, PIN hash, recovery code, saved playlists and their order and options, channels, favourites, groups, locks, and reminders.
- **Logo cache cap:** 50 MB, with LRU eviction. Startup clear removes logo files older than the cap policy allows. This keeps the first screen fast on slow networks while meeting the cache-clear requirement.

### 3.14 Settings (everything configurable)
- **Playback:** buffer, default quality, auto-quality, aspect ratio, subtitle style, default audio language.
- **Startup:** playlist to open, resume last channel, Home or Player on launch, auto-start on TV.
- **Navigation:** Back behaviour (default off), channel-change method (instant or OSD delay), number-entry timeout, color-button mapping.
- **Display:** theme, text size, high contrast, OSD timeout (default 5 s).
- **Safety:** Child Safe Mode, PIN, recovery, default lock behaviour, session-unlock duration.
- **Sleep and reminders:** sleep default, reminder lead time, reminders list.
- **Playlists and EPG:** manage playlists and priority, refresh interval, EPG source, clear cache.
- **Cache:** clear on exit toggle, clear on startup toggle, logo cache size, Clear now.
- **Advanced:** export/import settings and playlist URLs (file or QR), diagnostics (codec, bitrate, dropped frames, "Send report"), reset, Exit app.

### 3.15 Other
- Number-key channel entry and last-channel toggle.
- Info banner (channel name, number, logo, now/next, resolution, buffer state). Appears on change and on OK.
- Playlist health check: flag dead streams, with an option to hide them.
- Auto-retry on stream failure (a few tries, then the next variant), with a clear message instead of a blank screen.
- Voice search on Fire TV and Android TV where supported.
- Phone-only: picture-in-picture and background audio with lock-screen controls.
- Accessibility: TalkBack labels, text scaling, high contrast, captions respecting system settings.
- Privacy: no account, no analytics unless opted in, no ads.
- Profiles (v1.1): per-person favourites, recents, and safety level. Child profiles always have Child Safe Mode on.

## 4. Performance and Lightweight Rules

- First frame under 2 seconds cold start on a Fire TV Stick Lite, with startup cache clear enabled and a 50-playlist library.
- Channel change to first frame under 2 seconds on a typical connection.
- Handle 10,000+ channels without UI lag: paged, virtualised lists.
- EPG: 7 days for 5,000 channels, with incremental updates.
- Parse M3U and XMLTV in streamed chunks, never fully in memory.
- Load logos at the displayed size only.
- Pause background updates and EPG refresh on low-memory signals. Pause decoding of hidden video.
- Offline: cached channels and EPG stay usable. Show a clear message when streams are unreachable.

## 5. Security

- PIN stored as a salted hash. Recovery code stored hashed.
- Playlist URLs and credentials in encrypted storage. No plaintext secrets in logs or exports.
- Settings export excludes the PIN hash unless the user chooses to include it.

## 6. Legal and Store Compliance

- Player only. Do not bundle, promote, or link to specific paid or unauthorised channel sources.
- Store listing focuses on "play your own playlists." No suggested providers.
- Show a clear notice that content rights are the user's responsibility.
- Check Google Play and Amazon IPTV policies before launch. They change over time.
