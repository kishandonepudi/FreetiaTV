Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Summary

  • No account. No sign-in.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking SDKs.
  • Everything you set up stays on your device.

What is stored on your device

Your playlists, channel lists, favourites, groups, locks, reminders, programme guide data and settings are stored only on your device. Playlist links can contain passwords or tokens, so they are stored encrypted. Your PIN and recovery code are stored only as salted hashes, never as plain text.

Uninstalling the app deletes this data. Freetia TV turns off Android cloud backup, so this data is not copied to Google or Amazon servers.

Network requests

Freetia TV only connects to addresses you choose:

  • playlist links you add,
  • programme guide (EPG) links from those playlists or that you add,
  • the streams and channel logos listed in those playlists.

The GitHub (foss) build also downloads one public starter playlist on first run, from https://iptv-org.github.io/iptv/countries/in.m3u. You can delete it at any time. The store builds do not do this.

Those servers are run by third parties, who may see your IP address and request details as with any website. Their own privacy policies apply.

Exporting settings

If you export your settings, the file is saved where you choose. Your PIN hash is left out unless you choose to include it.

Children

Freetia TV collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
